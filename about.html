About Hallmark Furnitures

A neighbourhood furniture shop, built on word of mouth and a lot of sanding.

Our Story

Hallmark Furnitures has been serving the Ruaka area — right opposite Foresight Academy — with well-made furniture for the living room, bedroom, dining room, and home office. What started as a small showroom has grown mostly through TikTok and Facebook, where our pieces get shared, saved, and asked about every day.

This site exists because those platforms are great for discovery but not for detail. We kept hearing the same thing: people love what they see on our page, but can't find prices, sizes, or a quick way to ask before making the trip down. So we built a home base — one place to see our full range, get the specifics, and reach us directly.

What We're About

  • Built to Last

    Solid wood frames and sturdy joinery, made for daily use, not just good photos.

  • Straight Answers

    No hidden pricing games — ask and we'll tell you exactly what a piece costs and when it's ready.

  • Local & Personal

    Every customer can walk in, see the pieces in person, and talk directly to the people who sell them.
