# Hallmark furniture
Hallmark furniture is a small medium enterprise located along ruaka road opposite foresight academy that deals with selling furniture products, they currently use social media platforms to showcase their products and advertisements eg Tiktok, facebook

## Problem 
- They do not have a centralized platform to showcase their products and pricing info, make advertisements 
- Customers have to reach out via phone number to make simple enquiries which is a waste of time for customers who are just scrolling through social media and some client comments are never replied to eg when a client ask for price
- Business nowadays are relying on websites to centralize social media presence and showcase their products
## Identify users
### Target users
- **New homeowners and movers:** who recently purchased a home or relocated and need to furnish multiple rooms from scratch
- **first time apartment owners:** young professional or student  looking for space-saving, modular and budget friendly pieces
- **Remote and Hybrid Workers:** Professionals seeking ergonomic office chairs, standing desks, and multi-functional home-office setups.
- **Interior Design Enthusiasts:** Homeowners upgrading their decor or following aesthetic trends who value high-end visuals and 3D room planners.
- **B2B and Commercial Buyers:** Small business owners, interior designers, or offices sourcing bulk or trade-catalog furniture
### Users needs
- **Accurate Sizing & Fit:** Users need detailed dimensions (height, width, depth, clearance) and interactive tools (like 3D room planners or AR mobile previews) to ensure the piece fits physically and visually in their space.
- **Visual & Material Confidence:** Customers need high-resolution, multi-angle photos and videos, clear fabric/material texture close-ups, and user-generated photos in real homes to bridge the gap of not seeing the item in person.
- **Transparent Pricing & Financing:** Buyers look for clear upfront costs, total cost calculations (including shipping, taxes, and assembly fees), and flexible financing options (like Buy Now, Pay Later) before committing to high-ticket purchases.
- **Delivery & Assembly Clarity:** Users expect highly specific shipping timelines, clear delivery methods (curbside vs. white-glove inside delivery), straightforward assembly requirements, and return policy details.- **Trust & Social Proof:** Shoppers rely heavily on verified customer reviews that detail the furniture's long-term durability, comfort levels, and true-to-life colors.
### User pain points
- Clients that make simple enquiries like pricing have to reach out via phone and may receive no replies to social media platforms, also a waste of time
### User expectations
- Flawless visuals for the furniture
- Financial transparency
- Granular filtering and search
- Trust through buyer reviews
- Frictionless return and support

## Requirements
### User Requirements
- Browsing: Customers need to find products quickly using a clean, well-organized navigation menu. - High-Quality Visuals: Because users cannot touch the furniture, they rely on crisp, multi-angle photos to evaluate quality.Detailed 
- Product Specifications: Clear information prevents purchase hesitation and reduces returns.### Functional Requirements (By Page)
1. HomepageHero Banner: A visually striking section showcasing the brand's style, current promotions, or seasonal collections.
- Featured Categories: Quick links to major sections (e.g., Living Room, Bedroom, Office).
- Best Sellers / New Arrivals: A curated grid of popular items to spark immediate interest.
- About Snippet: A brief introduction to the brand's philosophy or craftsmanship.
2. Product Catalog / Gallery
- Grid Layout: A clean, organized display of products with clear pricing and names.
- Static Filtering & Sorting: Fast, client-side filtering by category, material, color, or price range.
- Status Badges: Visual labels for "In Stock," "Low Stock," or "Clearance."
3. Product Detail Page (PDP)
- Image Gallery: Multiple high-resolution images, including close-ups of textures and lifestyle/room settings.
- Product Dimensions: Clear measurements (width, height, depth) alongside a diagram if possible, so users can verify the fit in their home.
- Material & Care: Detailed descriptions of the wood, fabric, metal, and instructions on how to clean it.
- Call to Action (CTA): A prominent "Inquire Now" or "Where to Buy" button, as static sites usually route transactions to third-party marketplaces, physical stores, or contact forms.
4. Contact & Location
- Lead Form: A simple contact form for custom furniture inquiries or general questions.
- Business Details: Clear display of store hours, physical address, phone number, and email.
- Embedded Map: A static or interactive map showing the showroom location.
### Non-Functional & Technical Requirements
- High Performance & Speed: The site must load instantly. Images should be optimized using modern formats like WebP to prevent slow load times.
- Mobile Responsiveness: A flawless layout across smartphones, tablets, and desktops, as a large percentage of users browse home decor on mobile devices.
- SEO Optimization: Clean HTML structure, proper meta tags, and alt text for all images to ensure the site ranks well on Google
- Accessibility (WCAG Compliance): Strong color contrast, readable typography, and keyboard navigability for all users.
